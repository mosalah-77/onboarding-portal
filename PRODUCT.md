# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Mohamed selected Sites, React/TypeScript, D1 and private R2 storage.

## Users

A synthetic client supplies a website brief. A synthetic agency owner reviews inputs and approves scope. Visitors switch between demo personas.

## Product Purpose

Teach and demonstrate onboarding from missing information to an accepted, versioned brief and handoff.

## Operating Context

Each visitor has an isolated run with two client workspaces. Drafts, requests and exact-version approvals are the main tasks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Follow the approved spec. Identity is simulated; stored data and permissions are real. Synthetic data only. No AI, billing or external messages.

## Brand Commitments

Original interface and copy. No Halvern branding or proprietary material. Use Humanizer on published text.

## Evidence on Hand

This is an independent portfolio project with no claims of clients, adoption or measured savings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, labels, linked errors, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile and zoom support.
